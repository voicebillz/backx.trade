# CT posts for gazz.fun

The goal of every post is to make a scrolling degen STOP. On crypto Twitter you
have one line and about one second. Everything in this skill serves that.

## Hard rules (owner-set, never break)

- **English**, unless the owner explicitly asks for Norwegian.
- **No emoji. Ever.**
- **No "--" and no em-dashes.** Use a period and a new sentence instead.
- **No hashtags** (they read as 2019 marketing and suppress reach on today's
  algorithm).
- Never name the founder. If a first-person voice is needed, "I'm the founder"
  is the most that can be said.
- **Never state a partnership, listing, or third-party approval as done before
  it is confirmed.** Say "in talks with", "reached out to", "working to land".
  This is a trust and legal line, not a style choice. Same for anything
  price-related: no promises, no "guaranteed", nothing that reads as financial
  advice.
- Sincere over salesy. When delivering bad news (delays, issues), lead with
  understanding, explain the why plainly, end forward-looking.

## HOUSE FORMAT (owner 2026-10-14: "write them like this, I like this")

This is the default shape for every gazz.fun post. Deviate only when the owner
asks for something else.

1. **Post 1 = the result, numbers first.** One short hook line, a blank line,
   then the hard numbers on their own lines (one item per line, name + figure),
   then one short line that frames them ("All in the last 24 hours."). No
   explanation yet. This post carries the image.
2. **Post 2 = "How it works:"** as a reply in the thread. Four short lines max,
   each one plain fact, each under ~50 characters. Explain it like to a friend:
   what it is, what you get, why it's easier, how you get out.
3. **Post 3 = the bigger picture.** Open with a turn ("And it's not just
   memes."), then the breadth in one or two lines with real counts, then a
   forward close ("More funds coming.").
4. **First reply under the thread = the link only** (`gazz.fun`).
5. **Truth check before writing:** pull every number live (API / on-chain) right
   before drafting, and soften any claim the data doesn't back. Examples from
   the reference thread: "hundreds of asset classes" became "almost 200
   assets" (194 in the catalog), "rotated daily" became "rotated by AI as the
   market moves" (there is no fixed cadence). Say what period a number covers.
6. **Tone:** human, easy to read, short lines, lots of whitespace. No hype
   words, no promises about future returns, no emoji, no hashtags, no
   em-dashes, no "--".

## Algorithm mechanics that decide reach

These are platform facts, not style preferences:

- **No external links in the post itself.** X heavily downranks link posts.
  Put gazz.fun (or any URL) in the FIRST REPLY, or render it inside the image.
  When delivering a post, always hand the owner the link-reply text too.
- **The first hour decides everything.** Early replies and dwell time compound.
  Remind the owner to reply to early comments personally; each owner reply
  re-enters the post into followers' feeds.
- **Native media wins.** A post with a strong image or MP4 gets several times
  the reach of bare text. Every major post ships with one.
- **One post, one idea.** The algorithm rewards dwell + replies on a single
  clear message. If the material genuinely holds several ideas, make a thread
  where EACH tweet independently obeys the hook rule. Default to one post.

## Reach hygiene (owner, 2026-09-19 — from the X "under the hood" report)

The account itself is CLEAN (0 account labels — no shadowban). The only limiter
was `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL` on ~10% of posts (47/451 in one month), which hides those
posts from non-follower recommendations. That is a per-post spam flag driven by
posting patterns, and ~15 posts/day is what triggers it. Keep every post below
the filter:

- **Cut volume to quality.** ~15/day is too much. Fewer, stronger, non-repetitive
  posts lower the flag rate AND raise average reach. Quality over cadence.
- **Never put a link in the post body.** The URL goes in the FIRST REPLY or baked
  into the image — never the tweet text. (Already the rule above; the 47 flags
  mean it was slipping. Hold the line.)
- **No duplicate or near-duplicate text.** Every post gets its OWN hook and its
  own wording, even when it's the same news. Never re-post a template with small
  tweaks — that reads as spam.
- **Drop hashtags, trim `$GAZZ`/CTA repetition, and never "like & RT".**
- **Space posts out.** No bursts of several posts in a short window.
- **Ship the win-image / one-idea-per-post pattern** (like the Bulldog Mansion
  win post): native media, one clear message, URL in the reply.

## Anatomy of a post that travels

Write in this order of importance:

1. **The hook (line 1).** It must pass this test: shown completely alone, with
   everything below cut off, would a stranger stop scrolling? Patterns that
   work:
   - Bold claim: "Launchpad V2 was never about one chain."
   - Contrarian: "Most launchpads launch you into one pool. We think that's broken."
   - Curiosity gap: "There's a side of V2 you will never see."
   - Number-first: "20+ pools. One launchpad."
   Never open with corporate throat-clearing ("We are excited to announce",
   "Big news", "GM fam"). If the draft's first word is "We", rewrite the hook.
2. **Whitespace.** Blank line after the hook. Then short lines, one idea per
   line. A list of punchy fragments beats a paragraph every time. If a line
   wraps twice on mobile (roughly 40 characters), cut it.
3. **Concrete specifics.** Numbers make it real: "20+ pools", "dozens of
   contracts", "4% fee". Vague words ("many features", "huge news") make it
   skippable. If a real number exists, use it. If it doesn't, don't invent one.
4. **Insider access.** CT loves build-in-public. Framing work as "what you'll
   never see" or "behind the scenes" turns an update into a reveal.
5. **Honesty as the flex.** Admitting what is NOT done ("some haven't answered
   yet", "we underestimated X") reads as confidence and builds more trust than
   polish. gazz.fun's brand is the honest launchpad. Lean into it.
6. **Forward-looking close.** One short line that gives a reason to watch the
   account: "Stay tuned." / "This week." / "More soon." Never a begging CTA
   ("like and RT", "drop a comment").

Length: a single post should fit in one screen on mobile (roughly 500-900
characters).

## Anti-patterns (instant rewrite if any appear)

"We are excited/thrilled/pleased", "Big/Huge news", "GM", "fam", "WAGMI",
"to the moon", ALL CAPS sentences, more than one exclamation mark per post,
tagging big accounts unprompted, "$GAZZ" spam in every line, promising dates
that aren't locked (say "this week" only when the owner has said it), any
sentence a real person wouldn't say out loud to a friend.

## Voice

Confident, direct, slightly understated. The team is small and building hard;
let that show. Read the draft out loud: if it sounds like a press release,
rewrite it. If it sounds like a builder talking, ship it.

## The image

Follow the asset rules in CLAUDE.md, plus:

- **4K source minimum** (16:9 post image = 4096x2304). Render DOM/CSS posters
  with Playwright at `deviceScaleFactor: 4` on a 1024x576 viewport, then verify
  sharpness with a 1:1 crop before delivering.
- **Brand palette** (from web/src/styles/global.css): bg `#0a0812`, ink
  `#f4f6fb`, lime `#b8f43a`, purple `#8b5cf6`, teal `#38e8d0`. Fonts: Space
  Grotesk for display, Inter for body.
- **Design for X's re-encoding:** flat colors, hard edges, big type. Glow and
  blur sparingly. Keep auras and gradients away from text.
- **Max ~12 words of copy in the image.** One dominant message in large type,
  short chips for supporting facts. The image must land its point at feed size
  (~500px wide) and must make sense with the post text invisible, and vice
  versa. Put gazz.fun in the image (that's where the URL lives, not in the
  post).
- Video: always deliver MP4 (h264, crf 14-16) alongside any GIF; recommend
  MP4. X-header images are static only; X GIFs max 15 MB.

## Going viral on X — the playbook (owner 2026-11-01)

Distilled from Yeezy (@yeezycooks), "How to Go Viral on X: The Complete
Guide" (2026-11-01, x.com/yeezycooks/status/2116766191851032582), adapted to
the gazz.fun hard rules above. When this section and the hard rules disagree,
the hard rules win (no emoji, no hashtags, no links in the body, never claim
an unconfirmed partnership, no price promises).

Core idea: virality is not luck and not follower count. A post spreads when
people stop scrolling, read, and want to pass it on. Package the information,
create curiosity, give people a reason to share.

### 1. Hook first: write FIVE, ship the best one
Before choosing, always draft five hooks across these styles and pick the
strongest. A hook makes the value obvious; it never tricks. (Bracketed
values are placeholders: fill them with real, current numbers only.)
- Curiosity: "Nobody is talking about what just happened to holder rewards."
- Contrarian: "Most launchpads launch you into one pool. We think that's broken."
- Numbers: "We paid holders $[live total] in ETH. Nobody had to click claim."
- Story: "[N] months ago this was [then]. Today it's [now]."
- Direct value: "4 ways your trades pay you back on gazz.fun."
Weak → strong: "How to grow on X" → "Most people grow on X completely backwards."

### 2. Write for the share, not just the read
People repost what makes them look smart, says what they believe, surprises
them, makes them laugh, teaches them something, or starts a conversation they
want others in. "Here are some features" is skippable; "These 3 things pay you
every time someone else trades" is saveable. Ask: why would a holder forward
THIS to a friend?

### 3. Use formats that already work
- Comparison: two recognisable things side by side (claim vs auto-airdrop,
  a launchpad that keeps fees vs one that pays holders). People pick sides.
- List: "5 things you can do with GUSD." Easy to consume and save.
- Infographic: a fee flow, a timeline, a one-glance stat card (see the
  marketing-image skill).
- Personal / build story: what we shipped, what broke, what we learned.
- Question / debate: a SPECIFIC prompt, never "Thoughts?".

### 4. Numbers stop the scroll
"An expensive computer was delivered" vs "A $60M supercomputer delivered for
$50." Prefer real scale gaps, percentages, records, payouts, volumes. Every
number must be real and checkable (pull it live: /api/stats, distributions,
on-chain). If it makes people say "no way that's real", it's the angle — and
it must be real.

### 5. Visuals are the cheat code
Screenshots, charts, before/after, comparisons, short MP4s. The visual must
land the idea in about a second at feed size; the caption says why it matters.
Video: the first second is the hook, no intros, no logo openers (motion-video
skill). Native media always beats a link.

### 6. Short and punchy: HOOK → INFORMATION → TAKEAWAY
Two or three sentences can be the whole post. Cut long intros, repeated
points, adjectives and jargon. Every line earns its place.

### 7. Timing and trends: be early, bring an angle
Ride what people already discuss (Robinhood Chain news, a stock-token
listing, a big market day, a major launch) but never just repeat the
headline. Add the angle nobody has: our numbers, our mechanism, what it means
for holders. Early helps; the angle makes people stay.

### 8. Engineer the kind of engagement you want
- Interesting facts → reposts. Useful guides → bookmarks.
- Strong (honest) opinions → replies. Memes → likes and reposts.
- Specific questions → conversations. Infographics → saves and shares.
- Stories → people reply with their own.
Close with a specific prompt when a reply is the goal ("Which would you
rather hold for a year: X or Y?"), never "Thoughts?" and never "like & RT".

### 9. Run it as a system, not a guess
1. Research: what in our niche (CT, Robinhood Chain, tokenized stocks,
   GambleFi) is getting unusual engagement, what questions keep coming up.
2. Angle: "what can we say about this that hasn't been said?"
3. Create: five hooks → best format → real numbers → a visual.
4. Publish: clean formatting, link in the first reply.
5. Review after 24-48 h: impressions, engagement rate, reposts, replies,
   bookmarks, profile visits, NEW FOLLOWERS. 50K views that bring 500
   relevant followers beat 500K views that bring nobody. Keep a short note of
   what worked (hook style, format, time) and repeat it.

### 10. Distribution is part of the post
Stay for the first hour: reply to real comments (each owner reply re-surfaces
the post). Join relevant conversations, add real insight under larger
accounts in the niche, quote-post developments with our angle, turn a winner
into a thread / longer piece later, and revisit the topic when news lands.
Build genuine relationships with creators in the niche. NEVER engagement
groups or bought interactions (and remember the SPAM_HIGH_RECALL limits in
"Reach hygiene").

### 11. Mistakes that kill growth
Copying viral posts word for word · writing for everyone (our audience is
on-chain traders + degens + holders) · no hook · engagement bait · ignoring
analytics · giving up after one flop.

### 12. Pre-publish checklist (run it every time)
- [ ] Does line 1 alone make a stranger curious? (five hooks written?)
- [ ] Is the main idea understandable in one read?
- [ ] Does it give something interesting or useful?
- [ ] Is there a strong visual / MP4 where it would help?
- [ ] Are every fact and number real and current?
- [ ] Is every sentence necessary?
- [ ] Is there a reason to share, save or reply (specific, not begged)?
- [ ] Does it fit OUR audience?
- [ ] Hard rules: no emoji, no hashtags, no em dashes, no link in the body,
      nothing unconfirmed claimed, no price promises.
