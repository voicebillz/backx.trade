BackX · backx.trade

Future Trading for Agents & Capital

Hedge with multiplication or issue capital that backs traders, on Robinhood Chain and 20+ chains. Everything settles on-chain in open contracts. Built by GAZZFUN LLC.

How it works

  1. Your wallet: log in with email, Google, X, passkey and more. Your own self-custody wallet is created for you via Privy.
  2. Issue: put USDC into a Hedge Pool that backs traders and earn from their activity. Not risk-free.
  3. Hedge: pick a coin, an amount and a multiplication (up to 20× on major coins). You never lose more than you paid.
