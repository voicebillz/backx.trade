Hedge & Issuer Trading on Robinhood Chain

Hedge a coin with multiplication backed by real tokens, or issue USDC to back hedges and earn borrow fees. Self-custodial embedded wallets. No funding fees. Built by GAZZFUN LLC.

How it works

  • Hedge: profit when a coin rises. Up to 20× multiplication on major coins; a $100 stake at 5× is a $500 position. You never lose more than you paid.
  • Issue Reserve: supply USDC to a coin's reserve. It is lent to hedges and comes back first when they close.
  • Swap portal: same-chain swaps on Ethereum, Base and BNB Chain from your own wallet.
